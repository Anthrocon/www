All gamers know that the holiday season is one of the most exciting times; from waiting for deals to drop to curling up on a cold morning with your favorite game, we want to channel some of that excitement into what’s coming next for Anthrocon planning\!

## Registration

Pre-registration for 2027 is open while inventory lasts OR until our cutoff date in June 2027, whichever happens first. **After that, you will be able to purchase Day Pass memberships at the door** while inventory lasts. As mentioned before, we are limiting the number of memberships for Anthrocon 2027 to ensure venue and attendee safety. Get your registration in advance so you can guarantee your experience \- we’ll announce when we’re close to our cap\! All information will continue to be updated on our [**Registration page**](/registration) as registration continues to fill up. 

## Hotel Accommodations and Transportation

As with previous years, our current plan is to open our staggered hotel blocks (by membership type) starting at the beginning of February. All official hotel reservations can be accessed through the registration system once you have a paid membership, and with the following schedule:

* Ultrasponsors: February 2, 2027  
* Dealers: February 9, 2027  
* Supersponsors: February 16, 2027  
* General (Attending/Sponsor): February 23, 2027

**Expectation for hotel availability**: Put simply, there are many more attendees of all levels than there are rooms at our most popular hotels. While upgraded memberships such as Supersponsors will have better chances of booking these popular hotels in advance, but with a limited supply of rooms and everyone looking to book the same type of rooms, you may not get the exact booking you want. **Rest assured, every hotel room at our partner hotels is a comfortable stay**, and all the hotels outside the immediate con area will have dedicated transportation services, helping you fast travel between your hotel and the convention. We’re constantly working to expand these services so you can look forward to Anthrocon no matter which partnered hotel you stay at.

**Renovations at the Drury Hotel Pittsburgh**: The Drury Plaza Hotel Pittsburgh Downtown is always working to enhance your experience and help you Travel Happy. As part of our commitment to continuous improvement, the Drury will be undergoing renovations through July 2027\. For Anthrocon 2027 attendees, this means that fewer rooms will be available, but we’re working to increase inventory at neighboring hotels to ensure there’s a place for you to stay.

**Beware of fake hotel offers**: If anyone else tells you they have “official” hotel rooms, they are likely attempting to scam you. Anthrocon-rate rooms for 2027 are only available through our official housing provider link which is uniquely tied to your registration. If you booked at a hotel outside of the convention’s block, you will NOT be able to get the convention rate nor get any assistance from Anthrocon once the block opens, and are subject to the hotel’s standard policies.

More details will continue to be updated on our [**Hotel page**](/hotel).

## Dealers and Artists Alley

Dealers Room applications have now closed, and curation and selection will happen between November and January 2027, with approved applicants notified no later than mid-January (before Hotel openings). Artists Alley lottery signups will be announced later in 2027\.

## Staff and volunteer opportunities

If you have a few spare hours at the convention and like giving back to the community, while also getting some great experience and building connections, volunteering could be a great fit for you. If you’re willing to contribute your time before or at the convention, let us know by completing the Volunteering section in our [**Registration system**](https://reg.anthrocon.org/apply/volunteer)\!

## Events

Our Events team is still preparing and testing out all the forms and things, but we’ll announce when everything’s ready to go live\! If you’re planning to host any event or meetup, or take to the stage with your talents, make sure to apply early as our team will be going through many applications and contacting approved submissions. If you have any questions about an event or performance you want to host, reach out to [**events@anthrocon.org**](mailto:events@anthrocon.org).

## River Cruises

River Cruise tickets are available to purchase on our registration platform\! Whether you’re looking for awesome photo opportunities or want to enjoy a beautiful sunset on the river, these cruises are always a popular way to enjoy both the city of Pittsburgh and hanging out with fellow furries. You can find available tickets by going onto our Registration system and viewing the “Event Store”. Details about 2027 pricing and timeslots will be updated prior to tickets opening on the [**River Cruises page**](/anthrocon-river-cruises).

## Stay up to date with latest announcements

For the latest information at the convention, make sure to follow our official channels on BlueSky, Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), and our community channels such as the official Discord server for Anthrocon. You can find links to all those platforms on the bottom of our website pages\!
